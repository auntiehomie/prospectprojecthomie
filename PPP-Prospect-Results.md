## Notes & caveats

- Distances are straight-line (geocoded), not drive-time.
- Some entries share a filing/registered address with a related entity (e.g. Abbey Park at Mill River / Abbey Park LLC; Aqua Tots Management / Aqua Tots Farmington Hills) — these may be the same operating business under different legal names.
- A handful of phone/email matches came from third-party aggregators (RocketReach, cached search snippets) rather than the business's own site — these are flagged in the Notes column and should be spot-checked before outreach.
- For the 39 businesses still missing an email, a direct crawl of each company's actual website (not just search snippets) was run: most publish only a contact form or JS-obfuscated address, several domains are parked/expired or no longer belong to the business, and a few have no independent website at all. Details are in the Notes column per row — this rules out "we just didn't look hard enough" for most of these, though a manual call or an email-finder tool (e.g. Hunter.io Domain Search) may still turn up an address our crawl couldn't see.
- Businesses with no verifiable public phone or email are marked "—"; this reflects search limitations, not confirmation that none exists.
- Full underlying data (loan-level detail, forgiveness amounts) is in `PPP-Prospect-Results.csv` in this repository.
